# Scope and Limitations

This repository is a conceptual, computational modeling project. It is designed to explore how layered stress-response strategies might behave in a hypothetical synthetic organism, not to validate a real biological construct.

## Limitations

- Parameter values are assumed and may not correspond to actual biological measurements.
- The modeled mechanisms are inspired by known strategies in nature, but they are not direct copies of proven biological systems.
- The project does not claim that the proposed organism would survive or function in the real world.
- Environmental conditions are represented in an abstract or simplified way for simulation purposes.
- The framework is intended for exploratory investigation and discussion rather than deployment-ready engineering.

## Research interpretation

Any outputs from this project should be interpreted as model-derived hypotheses. The value of the project lies in the exploration of interaction patterns, resilience trade-offs, and emergent system behavior under stress.

## Recommended framing for readers

The correct reading of this repository is:

- speculative
- model-driven
- biology-inspired
- computationally exploratory
- not a validated real-world organism proposal
