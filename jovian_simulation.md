# 🪐 Jovian Orbital and Atmospheric Simulation Profile

## 📊 Environmental Boundary Layout
* **Orbital Zone Phase (Hours 0–24):** High vacuum environment (0 bars) with intense, unshielded magnetospheric radiation flux reaching 8,000 units.
* **Atmospheric Descent Phase (Hours 24–48):** Rapidly scaling hydrostatic pressures traversing from 0.5 bars down to a crushing 150 bars, with core thermal profiles reaching 350°C.

## ⚙️ Automated Protective State Transitions
* **Orbital Shielding Response:** The cell registers the 8,000-unit radiation threshold and activates its Tier 2 Cosmic Shield. Surface monomers immediately polymerize to absorb the ionizing flux and convert the rays into baseline metabolic energy. Simultaneously, the vacuum exposure triggers tardigrade-derived vitrification, locking the cellular core into a safe, dormant Tun state.
* **Atmospheric Descent Response:** Upon passing the cloud deck boundary, external radiation drops significantly. The cell automatically disassembles its dense polymer shield back into loose constituent parts to preserve energy. As the descent forces the environment past the 20-bar limit, deep-sea piezophilic fatty-acid optimization loops kick in, maintaining absolute membrane fluidity to prevent structural compression collapse.
* **Subsurface Core Metabolism:** Deprived of all solar illumination within the lower Jovian clouds, the cell activates its subglacial Antarctic chemoautotrophic engine, chemically breaking down local atmospheric sulfur compounds to maintain continuous ATP production.
