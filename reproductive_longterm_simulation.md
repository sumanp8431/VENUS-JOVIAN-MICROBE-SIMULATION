If your synthetic colony is left inside these planetary engines for thousands of generations, it will undergo Directed Evolution and Mutational Drift, permanently locking in unique physical changes:

### 🪐 1. The Venusian Cloud Strain (The Hyper-Light Floater)

* **The Result:** It will delete unnecessary, bulky genetic sequences to lighten its molecular mass. Over generations, the *Lactobacillus* chassis will evolve a highly gas-permeable, hollow cell envelope.

### 🌊 2. The European Benthic Strain (The Glass Rock-Crusher)
* **Long-Term Changes:** Locked in complete darkness at 1,200 bars of pressure for centuries, the cell will completely discard all structural paths related to light or radiation protection.
* **The Result:** The dynamic supramolecular shield will permanently deactivate. Instead, the cell wall will optimize into a thick, heavily calcified, crystal-like armor layer that bonds directly with its environment.
