### 🟢 1. The Active State (Venus Clouds / Europa Seafloor / Jovian Deep Decks)
When liquid solvents are present, the cell reproduces via Binary Fission.
* **The Challenge:** Normal cell division requires proteins like FtsZ to form a ring in the middle of the cell to pinch it in half. Under extreme heat (Venus) or crushing pressure (Jupiter/Europa), standard FtsZ rings warp and fail.
* **The Real-World Fix:** Your simulation assumes the integration of pressure-stable cell division proteins (chaperone-stabilized FtsZ) that keep structural division aligned, allowing the cell to clone itself every 4 to 12 hours depending on how much inorganic iron or sulfur fuel it harvests.

### 🔴 2. The Dormant State (Deep Space Vacuums / Solar Corona Boundaries)
When drifting in the high vacuum of solar orbits or unshielded space, moisture availability hits the 0.01 limit.
* **Reproduction Status:** Completely Suspended.
* **The Mechanism:** The tardigrade-derived TDP genes immediately trigger vitrification. The cellular interior glass-locks. All metabolic replication loops drop to 0%. The organism cannot divide or multiply in this state. It acts purely as a data vault, holding its genome perfectly frozen until it lands back into a hyper-hydrated matrix.

## ⏳ Part 3: The Long-Term Evolution of Simulated Lifestyles
If your synthetic colony is left inside these planetary engines for thousands of generations, it will undergo Directed Evolution and Mutational Drift, permanently locking in unique physical changes:

### 🪐 1. The Venusian Cloud Strain (The Hyper-Light Floater)
* **Long-Term Changes:** To avoid falling down into the lethal, super-heated lower furnace of Venus, the cell will naturally drift toward extreme minimalism.
* **The Result:** It will delete unnecessary, bulky genetic sequences to lighten its molecular mass. Over generations, the *Lactobacillus* chassis will evolve a highly gas-permeable, hollow cell envelope that acts as a microscopic biological hot-air balloon, keeping the colony permanently buoyant within the 50 km acidic sweet-spot layer.

### 🌊 2. The European Benthic Strain (The Glass Rock-Crusher)
* **Long-Term Changes:** Locked in complete darkness at 1,200 bars of pressure for centuries, the cell will completely discard all structural paths related to light or radiation protection.
* **The Result:** The dynamic supramolecular shield will permanently deactivate. Instead, the cell wall will optimize into a thick, heavily calcified, crystal-like armor layer that bonds directly with the iron basalt floor. The organism will look less like a bacterium and more like an animated, microscopic mineral crystal that spreads across hydrothermal vents like a living sheet of rust.
